The real switching problem

A new vendor can move the same problem.

Practice-management software gives every firm a framework. Your team still has to bridge the gaps between that framework and the way work actually happens.

  • The document is in one system and the decision is in another.
  • The client request never becomes a tracked action.
  • The call changes the job, but the workflow does not update.
  • Accountants rebuild client context before doing the real work.
  • Switching platforms retrains the team without fixing every handoff.

Do not trade one rigid operating model for another.

Questions firm owners ask

A custom platform without a blind leap.

Do we have to leave TaxDome, Karbon, or Canopy?

No. Keelwater can connect the platform you use, replace selected constraints, or replace practice management. The workflow decides.

Is this practice-management software?

No. Practice-management functionality can be part of the platform, but Keelwater can also connect communication, calls, documents, client accounting data, tax systems, workflows, and agentic execution.

Why is this possible now?

AI reduces the cost of building, integrating, and operating firm-specific software. It also lets the platform perform repeatable work, assemble context, and bring exceptions to people for review.

Will it replace professional judgment?

No. The platform is designed to prepare work, move context, and complete repeatable steps while keeping review and sign-off with the firm's professionals.

What happens after implementation?

The firm can operate the implemented platform without a required maintenance subscription. Optional ongoing support is available when the firm wants continued development, monitoring, or operations help.

How quickly should we see value?

The first 30 days are designed to map the workflow, establish the baseline, and connect the first high-value path. Timing and results depend on system access, scope, data quality, and adoption.

What does it cost?

Every implementation is scoped around the firm's workflow and systems. Keelwater discusses commercial fit after the initial strategy call rather than publishing a one-size-fits-all SaaS price.
