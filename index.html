The real problem

Your firm doesn't have an AI-awareness problem. It has an implementation problem.

The tools are already on your team's desks: ChatGPT, tax software, practice management, portals, bookkeeping. The work between them is still manual:

  • Chasing clients for documents
  • Re-entering the same data
  • Repetitive reconciliation
  • Status emails and manual reporting
  • Staff bottlenecks every busy season

The result: you can't grow without hiring more people.
