Where the hours go

The time doesn't leak inside your tax software. It leaks between the steps.

Engage, collect, prepare, review, sign, bill. Every handoff is someone copying data, chasing a client or answering "where's my return?" That's where we start.

EngageCollectPrepareReviewSignBill

Document chasing

Organizers pre-filled from last year, reminders that send themselves, and jobs that move to "ready to prep" the moment a client is complete.

Retyping into the return

W-2s, 1099s, K-1s and trial balances flow into the tax software. Staff review flagged fields instead of keying every line.

"Where's my return?"

One board showing where every return sits, with client updates that go out automatically as it moves.

Review ping-pong

Standard checklists and an AI pre-review that flags common errors and big year-over-year swings before a partner sees it.

Signatures and payment

Deliver, e-sign the 8879 and pay in one step, with reminders until it's done.

Advice left on the table

After filing, every return gets scanned for planning opportunities, so your best clients hear from you before tax season, not after.

Closed tax software? Still works.

Most tax platforms don't offer an open API. Where there's no clean connection, we set up automation that works the software the way a trained staff member would: filling in the fields you'd otherwise retype, with a person reviewing before anything is filed. You keep the software your team already knows.
