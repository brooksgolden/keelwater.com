Active design-partner pilot

Building the operating layer inside a modern tax & advisory firm

The firm was already unusually AI-forward: it used modern software and internal Claude skills. The limitation was not AI awareness. Its systems still operated independently, workflow state lived in multiple places, and staff manually coordinated the gaps between practice management, communication, spreadsheets and financial data.

Workstream 1

Client and project operating layer

ProblemThe firm has multiple working surfaces for client and project status. Practice management is intended to be the system of record, while spreadsheets, Slack and newer task-management tooling also contain operational truth. Owners cannot always see the true stage, blocker, owner and next action without reconstructing it manually.
BuildCreate a thin operating layer above and between the current systems that derives a reliable view of client work and lets natural communication (including Slack instructions) update the workflow without another manual tracker.

Keelwater layer: unified client & work view

Workstream 2

Multi-client QuickBooks / advisory context

ProblemRecurring advisory work requires staff to gather current books, payroll, prior returns, client history and other context. Opening and reconstructing that context client by client creates repetitive work.
BuildCreate secure, permissioned access to the relevant client financial context so the firm's existing advisory and AI process can begin with the information already assembled for human review.
