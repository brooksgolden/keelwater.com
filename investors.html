The thesis

Accounting does not have an AI-awareness problem. It has an implementation problem.

Most firms already know AI matters. The opportunity is making it work inside real tax and accounting workflows:

  • Client document intake
  • Preparation
  • Reconciliations
  • Close
  • Client communication
  • Research and reporting

The point is not to replace professional judgment. It is to remove repetitive work, so great accountants can serve more clients, respond faster, expand advisory and grow without hiring at the same rate.

Where the market is

Long-term partnership

Building for decades, not a three-year flip.

Keelwater's long-term ambition is to become a permanent ownership partner to great accounting firms. The best firms should keep what made them successful while gaining technology, growth capability, talent and capital that are difficult to build alone. As the platform develops, we expect to partner selectively with owners thinking about succession, liquidity or the next stage of growth.
