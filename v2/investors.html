The investment thesis

Services were historically constrained by labor. AI changes the economics.

Traditional service businesses have historically been difficult to scale: revenue growth usually requires proportional headcount growth. That limits margins and operating leverage.

AI changes that equation. When repetitive production work can be automated while professional judgment stays with the human, the same team can support more revenue - creating increasingly software-like economics inside a durable service business.

Keelwater is applying that thesis to accounting.

AI is turning historically low-margin service businesses into software-like investment opportunities.

Why accounting

A fragmented, recurring service market with a capacity problem.

Recurring client relationships

Tax, bookkeeping and CAS relationships repeat annually or monthly.

Labor-constrained growth

Good firms routinely hit hiring and busy-season capacity constraints.

Measurable workflows

Document intake, preparation, reconciliations, close and client communication can be measured before and after AI deployment.

Succession + consolidation

A fragmented owner-led market creates a natural platform-and-add-on acquisition model.

Founder and team

Commercial transformation is already proven. Accounting-specific execution is what we're validating now.

Founder Brooks Golden has spent eight years building growth and transformation systems inside B2B service businesses. Keelwater combines that commercial capability with an accounting-specific AI operating system and domain expertise assembled around the platform.

The founder's role is commercial transformation, platform strategy, capital allocation and acquisitions. The team is being built around complementary strengths: accounting operations, applied AI, and M&A / finance.

Long-term ownership

Building for decades, not a three-year flip.

Keelwater is being built as a long-term owner, not a financial engineering vehicle. The goal is to preserve what makes great firms valuable, then give them technology, commercial capability, talent and capital that are difficult to build independently. As the platform develops, we expect to partner selectively with owners thinking about succession, liquidity or the next stage of growth.
